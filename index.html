Meet Ms. Hyde

Every deck hides a face card nobody quite expects. Ms. Hyde is mine: the alter ego who steps out the moment the mask goes on. She has her habits, her tastes and her secrets, and she rarely shows her hand.

This little quiz puts you to the test. Five cards, five questions, and only one thing to prove: how well do you really know her?
